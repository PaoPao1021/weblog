On my first independent project, I easily confused taking the work seriously with building everything at once.

## Let version one prove one promise

Say who the project helps and what it helps them complete, then make the shortest version of that path work. Other features earn priority only after somebody actually uses the core loop.

## Make progress visible

A small version that opens in a browser exposes more truth than a growing plan. Real pages force decisions about copy, empty states, mobile screens, and details that imagination conveniently hides.

## Keep a way back

Small commits, repeatable builds, and clear change notes are not glamorous. They protect the confidence to keep making. The smaller the project, the easier it is to establish those habits early.
