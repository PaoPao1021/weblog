A useful learning note does more than move information elsewhere. It shortens the time needed to re-enter a problem.

## Begin with a question

Write down why the subject matters before collecting conclusions. The question tells you which details deserve attention and keeps the note from becoming an undirected archive.

## Preserve the decisions

If a note contains only the right answer, your future self cannot see the forks in the road. Failed attempts, reasons for rejecting them, and conditions of use are often the most reusable parts.

## Finish with a real task

A learning path should end in one small, complete piece of work. It does not need to impress anyone; it only needs to show that understanding has become ability.
